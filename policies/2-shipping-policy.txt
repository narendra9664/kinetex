SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. WHERE WE SHIP
We currently ship to India and the United States. The countries available at checkout are the countries we ship to.

2. ORDER PROCESSING
Orders are processed within 1-3 business days (excluding weekends and public holidays). You will receive a confirmation email when your order is placed and another email with a tracking number when it ships.

3. SHIPPING RATES AND DELIVERY TIMES
Our products ship from our fulfilment partner's warehouses. Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

India
- Standard shipping: FREE
- Estimated delivery: [7-15] business days

United States
- Standard shipping: FREE on orders over $50; [US SHIPPING PRICE] on orders under $50

Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays can happen due to customs, weather, carrier issues, peak seasons or public holidays.

4. ORDER TRACKING
Once your order ships, we will email you a tracking number. Tracking can take 2-5 business days to show updates after dispatch. If your tracking hasn't updated for more than 10 business days, please contact us.

5. MULTIPLE PACKAGES
If you order more than one item, your order may arrive in separate packages on different days. You will not be charged extra for this.

6. CUSTOMS, DUTIES AND TAXES
International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes or customs fees charged by the destination country. These charges are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the product price or shipping cost. If you refuse to pay these charges and the package is returned, the shipping cost and any duties are non-refundable.

7. WRONG ADDRESS
Please make sure your shipping address is complete and correct. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we will try to update it before the order ships.

8. LOST OR DAMAGED PACKAGES
If your package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, please check with neighbours and your local post office, then contact us within 7 days. If your order arrives damaged, see our Return and Refund Policy.
