RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Last updated: September 23, 2026

We want you to be happy with your MotionPluse order. Because our products are worn directly on the body or used for personal care, we accept returns only for items that arrive damaged, defective or incorrect.

1. WHAT WE COVER
We will replace or refund your item if it:
- Arrives damaged
- Is defective or does not work as described
- Is not the item you ordered (wrong product, size or colour sent by us)

2. WHAT WE CANNOT ACCEPT
For hygiene and safety reasons, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, choosing the wrong size, or items that have been worn, used or washed. Please check the product description and sizing information before ordering.

3. HOW TO REPORT A PROBLEM
Please email us at narendrakumar9664@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery with:
- Your order number
- Clear photos or a short video showing the problem
We will reply within 2 business days. In most cases you will not need to send the item back. If we do need it returned, we will give you instructions and cover the return shipping cost.

4. REPLACEMENTS AND REFUNDS
Once your claim is approved, we will send a free replacement or give you a full refund, including the original shipping cost, whichever you prefer (subject to stock). Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few extra days to appear on your statement.
Refunds are issued in the currency you paid in. We are not responsible for differences caused by exchange rates or bank fees.

5. ORDER CANCELLATIONS
You can cancel your order for a full refund within 12 hours of placing it by emailing narendrakumar9664@gmail.com. Once an order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

6. YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS
This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you.

7. CONTACT
Questions about an order? Email narendrakumar9664@gmail.com and we'll be happy to help.
